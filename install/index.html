Install Coco

EN FR

Add Coco the Axolotl to your device for quick and easy access, just like an app!

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

1

Open cocotheaxolotl.org in Safari

2

Tap the Share button ↑︎ (bottom of screen)

3

Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"

4

Tap "Add" — Coco now appears on your home screen!

Note: This only works in Safari. Chrome and other browsers on iOS don't support this feature.

Android (Chrome)

1

Open cocotheaxolotl.org in Chrome

2

Tap the three dots menu ⋮ (top right)

3

Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"

4

Tap "Install" — Done! Coco is now on your home screen.

Computer (Chrome / Edge)

1

Open cocotheaxolotl.org in Chrome or Edge

2

Click the install icon in the address bar (or the three dots menu → "Install Coco the Axolotl")

3

Click "Install" — Coco opens as its own window!
