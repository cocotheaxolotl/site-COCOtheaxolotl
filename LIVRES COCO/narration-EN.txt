Book: Whose Egg Is This? - A Coco the Axolotl Adventure

[COVER]
Whose Egg Is This?
A Coco the Axolotl Adventure

[INTRO]
One sunny morning, little Coco found a cracked eggshell by the river.

"Oh! Whose egg is this?" Coco wondered.
"I must find who it belongs to!"

And so, Coco set off on a big adventure to meet all the baby animals.

[ANIMAL 1/29] 🐱 Baby Kitten
Coco met a tiny kitten playing with a ball of yarn.
"Is this your eggshell, little kitten?"
The kitten purred and shook its head.
"No, silly! Kittens don't hatch from eggs! But keep looking!"

Did you know?
Kittens are born with their eyes closed. They can't see for the first 7-10 days!

[ANIMAL 2/29] 🐶 Baby Puppy
Next, Coco found a puppy wagging its tail.
"Is this your eggshell, little puppy?"
The puppy barked happily.
"Woof! No way! Puppies don't come from eggs! Try asking someone else!"

Did you know?
Puppies are born deaf! They can't hear anything for the first two weeks.

[ANIMAL 3/29] 🐰 Baby Bunny
Coco hopped over to a fluffy bunny nibbling a carrot.
"Is this your eggshell, little bunny?"
The bunny wiggled its nose.
"Hee hee! No! Bunnies don't hatch from eggs! But I do love Easter eggs!"

Did you know?
Baby bunnies are called kittens too! A group of baby bunnies is called a litter.

[ANIMAL 4/29] 🦄 Baby Unicorn
Coco spotted a baby unicorn with a sparkly little horn.
"Is this your eggshell, little unicorn?"
The unicorn shook its rainbow mane.
"Oh no! Unicorns are born with a sprinkle of magic, not from eggs!"

Did you know?
The unicorn is the national animal of Scotland!

[ANIMAL 5/29] 🐼 Baby Panda
A round baby panda was munching on bamboo.
"Is this your eggshell, little panda?"
The panda giggled.
"No no! Pandas don't come from eggs! But this bamboo is yummy — want some?"

Did you know?
Baby pandas are born pink and tiny — about the size of a stick of butter!

[ANIMAL 6/29] 🐬 Baby Dolphin
Coco swam to a baby dolphin splashing in the waves.
"Is this your eggshell, little dolphin?"
The dolphin did a flip.
"Haha! Dolphins are born in the ocean, not in eggs! Keep searching, friend!"

Did you know?
Baby dolphins can swim from the moment they're born!

[ANIMAL 7/29] 🐴 Baby Foal
A wobbly baby horse was learning to stand.
"Is this your eggshell, little foal?"
The foal stumbled and laughed.
"Neigh! Horses don't hatch from eggs! We stand up right after we're born!"

Did you know?
Baby horses can walk and run just a few hours after birth!

[ANIMAL 8/29] 🦋 Baby Butterfly
A baby butterfly was stretching its new wings.
"Is this your eggshell, little butterfly?"
The butterfly fluttered.
"Well... I did come from an egg! But it was a tiny, tiny egg on a leaf. That big shell isn't mine!"

Did you know?
Before becoming a butterfly, they're caterpillars! The change is called metamorphosis.

[ANIMAL 9/29] 🦖 Baby Dinosaur
Coco stumbled upon a baby T-Rex playing with a rock.
"Is this your eggshell, little dinosaur?"
The dino roared softly.
"RAWR! Dinosaurs did hatch from eggs, long ago! But that shell is too small for me!"

Did you know?
The biggest dinosaur eggs ever found were as big as a basketball!

[ANIMAL 10/29] 🦁 Baby Lion
A brave little lion cub was practicing its roar.
"Is this your eggshell, little lion?"
The cub tried to roar but it came out like a squeak.
"Mew! No! Lions are not born from eggs! But I'm the king of this jungle!"

Did you know?
Lion cubs are born with spots! The spots fade away as they grow up.

[ANIMAL 11/29] 🐻 Baby Bear
A fuzzy bear cub was licking honey from its paws.
"Is this your eggshell, little bear?"
The cub licked its lips.
"Nope! Bears are born in cozy caves, not eggs! Want some honey?"

Did you know?
Bear cubs are born during winter while mama bear is hibernating!

[ANIMAL 12/29] 🐘 Baby Elephant
A baby elephant was spraying water with its trunk.
"Is this your eggshell, little elephant?"
The elephant trumpeted and laughed.
"PFRRRT! No way! I was way too big to fit in any egg!"

Did you know?
Baby elephants weigh about 100 kg at birth — that's like a big refrigerator!

[ANIMAL 13/29] 🦒 Baby Giraffe
A tall baby giraffe was trying to reach a leaf.
"Is this your eggshell, little giraffe?"
The giraffe bent its long neck down.
"No! Giraffes don't come from eggs. But even as a baby, I'm already so tall!"

Did you know?
Baby giraffes are 1.80 m tall at birth — taller than most adult humans!

[ANIMAL 14/29] 🐧 Baby Penguin
A baby penguin was sliding on its belly on the ice.
"Is this your eggshell, little penguin?"
The penguin clapped its flippers.
"Yes, I did hatch from an egg! But my egg was kept warm by my daddy's feet! That shell isn't mine."

Did you know?
Emperor penguin daddies keep the egg warm on their feet for 2 months without eating!

[ANIMAL 15/29] 🐨 Baby Koala
A sleepy baby koala was clinging to its mama's back.
"Is this your eggshell, little koala?"
The koala yawned.
"Zzz... no... koalas ride in mama's pouch... not eggs... zzz..."

Did you know?
Koalas sleep up to 22 hours a day! That's almost the whole day!

[ANIMAL 16/29] 🦊 Baby Fox
A playful baby fox was chasing its own tail.
"Is this your eggshell, little fox?"
The fox spun around.
"Nope! I was born in a den underground! This tail is more fun than any egg!"

Did you know?
Baby foxes are called kits! They love to play-fight with their brothers and sisters.

[ANIMAL 17/29] 🦉 Baby Owl
A fluffy owlet was blinking its huge eyes.
"Is this your eggshell, little owl?"
The owl hooted softly.
"Hoo hoo! Yes, owls hatch from eggs! But mine was in a nest high up in a tree. That one's not mine!"

Did you know?
Owls can turn their heads almost all the way around — they can look behind them without moving their body!

[ANIMAL 18/29] 🐢 Baby Turtle
A tiny baby turtle was slowly crawling toward the sea.
"Is this your eggshell, little turtle?"
The turtle smiled.
"Yes! I hatched from an egg on the beach! But my shell was buried in the sand. That one isn't mine!"

Did you know?
Sea turtles return to the same beach where they were born to lay their own eggs!

[ANIMAL 19/29] 🐸 Baby Frog
A little frog was sitting on a lily pad.
"Is this your eggshell, little frog?"
The frog jumped up.
"RIBBIT! Frogs don't come from eggs like that! We start as tiny dots in jelly in the water! I was a tadpole first!"

Did you know?
Frogs go through metamorphosis — from egg to tadpole to frog!

[ANIMAL 20/29] 🐮 Baby Highland Cow
A fluffy baby Highland cow peeked through its long bangs.
"Is this your eggshell, little cow?"
The calf swished its fluffy hair.
"Moo! No way! I just have the best hair of all the babies here!"

Did you know?
Highland cows come from Scotland and their long hair keeps them warm in cold weather!

[ANIMAL 21/29] 🦩 Baby Flamingo
A baby flamingo was standing on one leg (or at least trying to).
"Is this your eggshell, little flamingo?"
The flamingo wobbled.
"Yes, I hatched from an egg! But mine was on a big mud nest. And look — I'm grey, not pink yet!"

Did you know?
Flamingos are born grey! They turn pink from eating tiny shrimp.

[ANIMAL 22/29] 🦥 Baby Sloth
A baby sloth was hanging upside down from a branch, veeeery slowly.
"Is this... your eggshell... little sloth?"
The sloth blinked.
"Nooooo... sloths... don't... come... from... eggs... we just... hang... around..."

Did you know?
Sloths are so slow that algae grows on their fur, making them look green!

[ANIMAL 23/29] 🦦 Baby Otter
A baby otter was floating on its back holding a little shell.
"Is this your eggshell, little otter?"
The otter giggled.
"No! Otters are born in the water! But I do love collecting shells! Can I keep it?"

Did you know?
Sea otters hold hands while sleeping so they don't drift apart!

[ANIMAL 24/29] 🦔 Baby Hedgehog
A tiny baby hedgehog was curled up in a ball.
"Is this your eggshell, little hedgehog?"
The hedgehog uncurled slowly.
"No! I was born soft and tiny. My spikes grew later! Be careful though — I'm a little prickly!"

Did you know?
Baby hedgehogs are born with soft spines hidden under their skin!

[ANIMAL 25/29] 🦙 Baby Llama
A fluffy baby llama was prancing around proudly.
"Is this your eggshell, little llama?"
The llama tossed its fluffy head.
"HA! Llamas don't hatch from eggs! We stand up and run right after we're born! Pretty cool, right?"

Did you know?
Llamas hum to talk to each other! A mama llama hums to her baby all the time.

[ANIMAL 26/29] 🐥 Baby Duckling
A yellow duckling was paddling in a puddle.
"Is this your eggshell, little duck?"
The duckling quacked.
"QUACK! Yes, I came from an egg! But mine was in a nest by the pond! That shell isn't mine!"

Did you know?
Ducklings can swim within hours of hatching! They follow the first thing they see.

[ANIMAL 27/29] 🐤 Baby Chick
A round little chick was pecking at seeds.
"Is this your eggshell, little chick?"
The chick cheeped.
"PEEP PEEP! I hatched from an egg too! But mine was warm under mama hen! That one looks different from mine!"

Did you know?
Baby chicks start peeping inside the egg before they hatch!

[ANIMAL 28/29] 🐑 Baby Lamb
A woolly little lamb was jumping in a meadow.
"Is this your eggshell, little lamb?"
The lamb bounced.
"BAA! No! Lambs don't come from eggs! We come with fluffy wool and springy legs!"

Did you know?
Baby lambs can recognize their mama's voice from birth!

[ANIMAL 29/29] 🐉 Baby Dragon
A baby dragon was blowing tiny smoke rings.
"Is this your eggshell, little dragon?"
The dragon puffed.
"Hmm! Dragons DO hatch from eggs! Big, sparkly eggs! But that shell isn't sparkly enough to be mine!"

Did you know?
In many stories, dragon eggs need fire to hatch!

[ENDING]
Coco was tired. So many baby animals, but still no owner for the eggshell. Walking slowly back home along the river, Coco carried the shell on top like a little hat.

Then — Mama Axolotl was standing by the water, and in her arms was the tiniest, cutest baby axolotl ever!

"Mama! Whose egg was this?"

Mama smiled softly. "That's your little sister's eggshell, Coco. She hatched this morning! Say hello to Lili!"

Baby Lili looked up at Coco with big sparkly eyes and giggled.

Coco laughed and kept the shell on top. "I'm keeping it! It's my special hat now!"

And from that day on, Coco wore the eggshell like a tiny explorer helmet — showing baby Lili all the wonderful animal friends along the way.

The End!
