## Tips for best results

1. **Always upload your Coco reference image first** — this is the #1 way to keep consistency
2. **After generating each image**, check: 3 fingers + 1 thumb (Mickey Mouse style)? Pink sneakers? Round pink ears on each side of head? Eggshell hat?
3. **If ChatGPT drifts from the design**, remind it: "Look at my reference image again. Coco has 3 fingers and 1 thumb on each hand like Mickey Mouse, pink sneakers, and small round pink ears on each side of the head."
4. **Generate in order** — ChatGPT keeps context from the conversation, so the style gets more consistent as you go
5. **Never say "feathered" or "gills"** in any custom prompt — these words produce wrong results
6. **Sneakers hide the toes** — much easier for AI to draw consistently than bare feet with 5 toes
