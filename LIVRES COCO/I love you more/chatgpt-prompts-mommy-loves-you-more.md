## Style Anchor (à inclure dans TOUS les prompts)

```

Coco is a small pink axolotl walking upright on two legs, with a wide happy smile, big round dark eyes, smooth soft pink body, small round pink ears/gills on each side of the head, cartoon-style hands with 4 digits like Mickey Mouse, wearing cute pink sneakers, and a cracked white eggshell on top of the head like a little hat (Calimero style).

Mama is a taller pink axolotl, same species as Coco, same gentle style, with a warm and loving expression. She also walks upright. She does NOT wear the eggshell hat.

Square format, 1:1 ratio.
```
