PUZZLE IMAGES FOLDER
====================

Add your puzzle images here in 9:16 portrait format (e.g. 900x1600 px).

Accepted formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp

Naming: use simple names without spaces, e.g.:
  coco-beach.jpg
  coco-underwater.jpg
  coco-christmas.jpg

The puzzle game will automatically detect all images listed in puzzle/index.html.
To add a new image to the game, open puzzle/index.html and add its filename
to the IMAGES array near the top of the <script> section.
